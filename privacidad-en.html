Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Fundamental Commitment to Privacy

At ReWine (developed by JACOOR), your privacy and data confidentiality are our highest priority. We engineered the app following the principle of Privacy by Design, ensuring that your wine tasting journey remains entirely under your personal control.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

ReWine DOES NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

  • We do not collect email addresses, names, phone numbers, or account profiles.
  • We do not collect Advertising Identifiers (IDFA) or Vendor Identifiers (IDFV).
  • We do not integrate invasive analytics SDKs, behavioral trackers, or ad networks (Tracking = NO).
  • We never track your background location or maintain movement histories.

3. Strictly Local Storage

All information entered into ReWine is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage (iOS / iPadOS local app sandbox):

  • Technical Tasting Sheets: Wine name, winery, product type, effervescence, color, vintage, grape varieties, venue, sommelier name, sensory impressions, sommelier level, and scores are stored locally.
  • Wine Photography: Captured photos of bottles, labels, glasses, corks, or pairings are compressed and saved in the app's private documents directory, preserving 100% of their original aspect ratio.
  • iCloud Backup: If iCloud backup is active on your device, your data is securely and encrypted backed up to your personal Apple account without access by ReWine or third parties.

4. Transparent Device Permissions

ReWine only requests system permissions when strictly necessary to fulfill user-initiated actions:

  • Location When In Use (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription): An optional permission used exclusively when you tap «Auto GPS» to identify the winery, restaurant, or municipality of your tasting. Geocoding is resolved locally on the device using Apple services. Coordinates are never sent to external servers, background tracking is not used, and manual or blank options are always available.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription & NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription): Requested solely when you tap the microphone button to dictate sensory tasting notes (visual, olfactory, or palate). Speech recognition runs 100% locally on your device via Apple's native framework. ReWine NEVER stores audio files, does not record in the background, and never transmits voice recordings to external servers.
  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Requested solely to allow you to take photos of wine bottles, labels, glasses, or pairings during tastings. Photos are processed and saved locally.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Requested solely to let you attach existing photos from your camera roll to tasting records.

5. Exported PDF Documents and Sharing

Tasting sheets formatted as PDFs are generated entirely on your device using iOS rendering engines. They feature vintage parchment styling in the active language, preserving original photo aspect ratios and sommelier signatures. Files are only shared when you explicitly initiate an action via iOS sharing menus (AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or AirPrint).

6. Age Rating & Responsible Drinking

ReWine is a technical, educational, and sensory notebook intended for wine enthusiasts and certified sommeliers. It strictly complies with Apple App Store guidelines and does not sell, distribute, or promote alcoholic beverage purchases.

Age Advisory & Responsible Enjoyment: The application holds an official 17+ rating on the Apple App Store. ReWine is intended exclusively for individuals of legal drinking age (+18 in most jurisdictions). We actively champion moderation and wine culture. Excessive alcohol consumption is harmful to health.

7. In-App Purchases and Secure Payments via Apple

ReWine offers an optional ReWine PRO lifetime unlock via non-consumable In-App Purchase. All financial transactions and payments are handled directly, securely, and exclusively by Apple using the native StoreKit 2 framework.

  • Zero Access to Financial Information: ReWine never collects, stores, or accesses credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing details.
  • On-Device Cryptographic Verification: Your lifetime purchase entitlement is cryptographically validated on-device using Apple-signed JWS receipts.
  • No Third-Party Intermediaries: We do not use third-party payment gateways or share purchase telemetry with advertising networks.
