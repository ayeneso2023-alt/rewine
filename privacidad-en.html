Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Fundamental Privacy Commitment

At ReWine (developed by JACOOR), the privacy and confidentiality of your data are our highest priority. We engineered the app following the Privacy by Design principle, ensuring that your tasting journey and enological notes remain under your exclusive control at all times.

2. Personal Data Collection

ReWine DOES NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

  • We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or account registration details.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or vendor identifiers (IDFV).
  • We do not integrate invasive analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking tools (Tracking = NO).
  • We do not track your location in the background or store location histories.

3. Strictly Local Storage

All data recorded in ReWine is stored exclusively within the internal memory of your device (iOS / iPadOS local sandbox):

  • Tasting Technical Sheets: Wine name, winery, product type, effervescence, color, vintage, grape varieties, tasting location, taster/sommelier name, full sensory analysis, and rating are saved locally.
  • Enological Photos: Images taken of bottles, labels, glasses, corks, or pairings are compressed and stored in the application's private documents directory, maintaining their original dimensions and proportions.
  • iCloud Backup: If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone or iPad, your records are backed up in encrypted form to your personal Apple account, inaccessible to ReWine or third parties.

4. Device Permission Usage

ReWine only requests system permissions when strictly necessary to provide the features initiated by the user:

  • Location While Using App (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription): Optional permission requested only when the user selects «Auto GPS» to log the location, winery, or restaurant of the tasting. Coordinates are resolved directly on-device using Apple's geocoding service. ReWine never uploads coordinates to external servers, never tracks in the background, and always provides options to enter locations manually or leave them blank.
  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Requested exclusively to allow you to photograph bottles, labels, glasses, or pairings during tastings. Photos are processed and stored locally.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Requested solely to allow you to import existing photos from your device's photo library into your tasting notes.

5. Exported PDF Documents

Tasting reports in PDF format are generated entirely on your device using the native iOS graphics engine. Formatted in vintage editorial styling, they preserve the original aspect ratio of your photos and display the taster name and location. PDF files are only transferred when you choose to share them voluntarily via the standard iOS share sheet (AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or AirPrint).

6. Age Rating & Policy Compliance

ReWine is a cultural, technical, and sensory cataloging tool designed for wine enthusiasts and professional sommeliers. It strictly complies with Apple App Store guidelines and does not sell, distribute, or promote the commercial sale of alcoholic beverages.
