The Art of Wine Tasting in Your Pocket with ReWine

The ultimate companion for wine lovers and sommeliers. Record complete tasting notes with auto GPS location, vintage wheel, 64+ grape variety catalog, sensory aroma wheel, and vintage parchment A4 PDF exports with perfectly proportioned photos.

Guide & Support
🌍

Fully Multilingual (7 Languages)

Available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, and Simplified Chinese. Choose your language at launch or switch dynamically anytime with certified sommelier terminology.

📍

GPS Tasting Location & Taster Name

Log your tasting venue effortlessly with three flexible modes: Auto GPS with one tap (winery or restaurant), manual text entry, or leave blank. Customize the evaluator/sommelier name for your official PDF sheets.

🍇

64+ Grape Variety Catalog

Comprehensive ampelographic catalog featuring 64 world and Iberian grape varieties (34 red and 30 white), with instant search and autochthonous translations (Grenache, Mourvèdre, Carignan, Alvarinho, Pinot Grigio, etc.).

🍷

3-Tier Professional Wine Classification

Structured 3-level categorization: Product Type (wine, fortified/dessert, low-alcohol, non-alcoholic, aromatized, must), Effervescence (still, semi-sparkling, sparkling), and Color (red, white, rosé).

📅

Vintage Wheel & Defect Detection

Spin the native harvest year wheel (1900 to present). Detect enological faults (cork taint/TCA, oxidation, reduction, brett, volatile acidity) and record custom defects under "Other".

🌸

Aroma Wheel & Glossary

Explore sensory descriptors categorized across 14 aroma families (citrus, white fruit, red fruit, floral, spices, oak, minerals). Identify subtleties both on the first nose and after swirling.

📸

Categorized Photo Gallery

Tag photos across 8 dedicated categories: Glass, Cork, Label, Bottle, Food Pairing, Winery, Vineyard, and General. Enjoy local high-resolution storage and full-screen inspection.

📄

A4 PDF Export with Aspect-Fit Photos

Generate vintage parchment tasting sheets formatted for A4 print and sharing. Photos strictly preserve 100% of their original proportions without stretching or distortion, framed in elegant gold.

🔒

100% Private, Secure & Offline

Operates entirely offline, perfect for cellars, vineyards, and tastings without cell service. No mandatory accounts, zero ad tracking, and secure encrypted iCloud backups.
