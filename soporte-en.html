Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the Sommelier Level work and what are the requirements for each tier?

ReWine's gamified progression evaluates your wine journey across 5 mastery tiers:
• Beginner: Starting from 1 tasted wine.
• Enthusiast: 20+ wines, 4+ Appellations/PGIs, and 3+ countries tasted.
• Intermediate: 50+ wines, 7+ Appellations/PGIs, and 5+ countries tasted.
• Advanced: 100+ wines, 15+ Appellations/PGIs, 7+ countries, and 2+ product types.
• Master of Wine: 200+ wines, 30+ Appellations/PGIs, 12+ countries, and 3+ product types.
Tapping the Sommelier Level card in the «Statistics» tab opens an interactive sheet with crests, progress percentage, expert tips, and exact milestones needed to reach the next tier.

How does voice dictation work for tasting notes and is it private?

In the visual, olfactory, and palate observation fields, you will find a microphone icon. Tap it and speak naturally; ReWine will transcribe your impressions in real time. Dictation is processed 100% locally on your device using Apple's native Speech framework. ReWine never stores audio recordings or sends voice data to external servers, guaranteeing total privacy.

What is the difference between primary, secondary, and tertiary aromas on the wheel?

ReWine's aroma wheel strictly adheres to international sommelier standards:
• Primary Aromas: Derived directly from the grape variety and terroir (citrus, stone fruit, tropical fruit, berries, floral, herbal, and balsamic notes).
• Secondary Aromas: Developed during alcoholic/malolactic fermentation and lees contact (dairy, fresh butter, pastry, and brioche).
• Tertiary Aromas: Created during oak barrel aging, solera aging, and bottle maturation (spices, vanilla, toasted wood, dried fruits, leather, and evolution minerals).

How do advanced filters and grape variety normalization work?

In «My Journal», filter by 7 official technical dimensions: 1) Product Type, 2) Character/Effervescence, 3) Color, 4) Vintage, 5) Appellation/PGI, 6) Country, and 7) Grape Varieties. The variety filter automatically normalizes names: for instance, wines listed as 'Tempranillo 5%' or 'Tempranillo 10%' are grouped cleanly under 'Tempranillo', eliminating duplicates and percentage noise.

What wine regions are included in the world catalog?

The ReWine catalog encompasses over 400 official Appellations, PGIs, and wine regions worldwide. It includes comprehensive coverage of Spain, France, Italy, Portugal, Germany, the USA, Argentina, Chile, South Africa, Australia, New Zealand, plus recently added Hungary (Tokaj, Eger, Villány, etc.) and Lebanon (Bekaa Valley, Batroun, Jezzine, etc.), with flags and localized names.

How are PDF sheets exported and how are photos rendered?

From any tasting entry, tap «Export to PDF». ReWine generates an A4 vintage parchment editorial sheet with luxury gold borders in the currently active language. Attached photographs are scaled using exact aspect ratio calculations, preserving 100% of their original proportions without cropping or distortion.

How does GPS location logging work and how is privacy handled?

In the technical section of each tasting, choose between: Auto GPS (resolves winery, restaurant, or city name locally using Apple's location framework without tracking background routes), Manual Typing, or Leave Blank. Coordinates are never sent to third-party servers.

How do I change the app's language?

ReWine is 100% localized into 7 languages (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, and Simplified Chinese). Select your language during onboarding or change it anytime by tapping the flag pill button in the top navigation bar of «My Journal».

Can I use ReWine without an internet connection?

Yes, completely offline. ReWine is built on a local-first architecture for underground cellars, barrel rooms, or remote vineyards. If iCloud backup is enabled on your iPhone or iPad, your tastings and photos are backed up securely and encrypted in your personal Apple iCloud storage.
