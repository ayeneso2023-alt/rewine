ReWine Technical Support

Official help center, FAQ, and guide to advanced enological features.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does language selection work and what languages are supported?

ReWine is 100% localized into 7 languages: English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, and Simplified Chinese. The language selector appears upon launch to set your preference, and you can switch languages anytime by tapping the flag pill button in the top navigation bar of "My Journal".

How do I log tasting location and how does automatic GPS work?

In the technical sheet of any tasting, choose from three location modes: Auto GPS (automatically identifies the winery, restaurant, or city using local on-device reverse geocoding with no background tracking), Manual text entry, or Leave blank.

How does PDF export work and how do photos render?

From any tasting sheet or detail view, tap the PDF export button. ReWine compiles an editorial A4 document on vintage parchment paper. All attached photos render in high resolution preserving 100% of their original proportions (portrait, landscape, or square) without stretching or distortion, elegantly framed in gold.

How do I select the harvest vintage and grape varieties?

For the vintage, use the native spinning wheel picker covering years 1900 to the present. For grape varieties, browse an expanded catalog of over 64 world varieties (34 red and 30 white), with instant search and authentic local names (e.g., Mourvèdre for Monastrell, Grenache for Garnacha, Alvarinho for Albariño).

What are the 3 tiers of wine classification?

ReWine categorizes wines across: 1) Product Type (Wine, Fortified/Dessert, Aromatized, Partially Fermented Must, Low-Alcohol, and Non-Alcoholic Wine); 2) Effervescence (Still, Semi-sparkling, and Sparkling); and 3) Color (Red, White, and Rosé).

Can I log custom wine defects beyond standard flaws?

Yes. In addition to standard defects (cork taint/TCA, premature oxidation, reduction, brettanomyces, volatile acidity, light-struck, excess SO2, and refermentation), you can check the "Other" box to type any custom flaw or aromatic anomaly.

Can I use ReWine without internet connectivity?

Yes, absolutely. ReWine is built on a 100% local, offline-first architecture. It works flawlessly in underground barrel cellars, wine fairs, or remote vineyards. If iCloud Backup is enabled on your Apple device, all tasting records and photos are safely and privately backed up to your personal iCloud storage.
